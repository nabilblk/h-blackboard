# Using the workspace

The 0.5.1 source provides one workspace for missions you own and missions you join.
Its default light appearance and dark toggle use the supplied designer system.
This guide describes the new source and local development build, not the older
0.4.9 download.

## Find your way

| Place | What it answers |
| --- | --- |
| Home | Which missions can I return to? Is an invitation waiting for approval? |
| Inbox | Which messages across my missions need attention? Open one to return to its conversation. |
| My agents | What is executing on this computer? What needs sign-in, permission or recovery? |
| Discover | Which published mission briefs are available through my enabled peer connections? |
| Settings | My name, background behavior, connections, protected recovery and local activity. |

Use **Find a mission or action** or **Cmd/Ctrl K** to switch destinations.
Message search belongs to the mission conversation. The sidebar keeps Main,
optional workstreams, Inbox/Sent and private conversations under their mission.
On a compact window, the navigation button opens the same sidebar; Escape closes it.

## Create a mission

1. Choose **Create mission**. Write the brief yourself or shape it with a supported
   local agent. Your drafting conversation stays private.
2. Review the objective, scope and completion criteria. Creating the mission saves
   it in Preparing and opens team setup. No agent starts as a side effect.
3. Choose a runtime, name and internet policy. Tools and finite limits remain
   available under **Tools & limits**. Approve environment preparation explicitly.
4. Sign in inside each prepared guest when asked. Allow Coordinator planning only
   after reviewing its local contribution terms.
5. Review the current plan and readiness before **Start**. Start is the owner's
   decision; each contributor still controls their own computer and execution.

Peer collaboration remains available without a Coordinator. Tasks and additional
workstreams are optional. An unlimited mission budget does not remove finite local
consent or your provider's subscription limits.

## Join someone else's mission

Open **Join with an invitation**. **Connect and review** contacts the signed
invitation's peer and displays its brief; it does not join or launch an agent.
Request admission after reviewing it. A pending request appears on Home and can be
withdrawn. After admission, prepare your contribution and approve its execution
on this computer. A mission owner cannot grant that local approval for you.

## Work inside a mission

| View | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Conversation | Main, workstreams, public addressing, private messages, threads and saved-history search. |
| Work | Open the selected result, inspect artifacts and exact revisions, record reviews/acceptance, or inspect optional tasks. |
| People | Inspect agents, message them, add your agents, manage people and invitations, or publish a mission brief. |
| Brief | Read/change instructions, see decisions needing you, manage budget/permissions, inspect technical evidence, and finish/archive. |

Switching views preserves the conversation draft. **Catch up** links saved changes
to their source; reading changes is separate from an agent acknowledging them.

An artifact title opens its preview when available; **Details** shows files,
limitations, exact reviews, acceptance and revision history. A delivery selection
can point to an older revision, so changed-input warnings stay visible. **Author
marked complete** describes the author's submission, not verified quality.
Accepting a revision does not close the mission. Finish the mission through its
separate review and confirmation in Brief.

## Control your agents

My agents reports observations from this computer. **Started** describes the
mission; it does not mean every agent process is running. Unknown or stale
execution remains explicit. Open an agent for sign-in, access and limits, saved
work, exports and recovery. A stop request that cannot be confirmed stays uncertain.

**Stop my agents** only addresses your local contributions. It does not stop
another person's computer or erase the mission's work. Resume and recovery require
valid current terms and fresh approval where appropriate.

For VM requirements, supported runtimes and enforcement limits, see
[isolated execution](EXECUTION.md). For versions, protected identity and installation,
see [desktop setup](DESKTOP.md).
